By objective · what was tested and what worked

ObjectiveBest-performing channelKey message and takeaway
Direct App Store acquisition Google App Campaign drove 40 of 81 Klaviyo-attributed installs, followed by iOS App Store campaigns with 21. Together, they generated 61 of 81 installs. On Google Play, “Check Your Passwords” paired with “You are the password—no master password to remember” was the strongest message.
Retargeting Google Display led site retargeting with 83 sessions and two downloads, compared with 64 sessions from Meta visitor retargeting. Meta iOS retargeting drove 46 App Store visits. TikTok generated 448 clicks but no installs. For site retargeting, “Kills the master password” generated 59 clicks and both downloads, outperforming the product-offer message. For App Store retargeting, “Simple and free” drove six store visits from seven clicks at a 1.19% CTR. If we continue TikTok, “Sync Your Devices” UGC is the strongest option, with 64 clicks and a 3.4% CTR.
PR traffic Meta clearly outperformed Reddit, generating 183 clicks versus 50 at a lower CPC of approximately $0.10. On Meta, “Kills the master password” drove 176 of 183 clicks and 72 landing-page views. On Reddit, “You are the password. Your face is the key” delivered the strongest CTR and generated 31 of 50 clicks.
Google Search The Search Volume RSA delivered 82 clicks, four download clicks, and 42 website sessions. Exact-match [password manager] accounted for approximately 76–80 clicks and all 4 download clicks. The strongest headlines were “No Master Password” and “Unlock With Face ID.”
YouTube UGC YouTube Shorts generated 137 website sessions and six download clicks, making it the strongest tagged website traffic source this week, although it did not produce any Klaviyo-attributed installs. “Mom as IT” led with 67 clicks and three download clicks, followed by “Airport Password” with 51 clicks and two. Performance was driven by the video concepts rather than the RSA copy.
