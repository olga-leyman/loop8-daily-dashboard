By objective · what was tested and what worked

ObjectiveBest-performing channelKey message and takeaway
Direct App Store acquisition Google App Campaigns are still the only paid source of accounts: 24 of 50 (22 Play, 2 iOS App Store). On Google Play, “Check Your Passwords” paired with “You are the password—no master password to remember” was the strongest message.
Retargeting Google Display led site retargeting with 225 sessions (156 + 69 from the 1-day list), compared with 112 sessions from Meta visitor retargeting. Meta iOS retargeting drove 157 App Store visits. TikTok generated 534 clicks but no installs. For site retargeting, “Kills the master password” generated 59 clicks and both downloads, outperforming the product-offer message. For App Store retargeting, “Simple and free” drove six store visits from seven clicks at a 1.19% CTR. If we continue TikTok, “Sync Your Devices” UGC is the strongest option, with 64 clicks and a 3.4% CTR.
PR traffic Meta clearly outperformed Reddit, generating 291 clicks versus 81 at a lower CPC. On Meta, “Kills the master password” (PR | Canva 3) drove 282 of 291 clicks and 111 landing-page views. Reddit still had the stronger CTR, with 81 clicks to the AP News article.
Google Search The Search Volume RSA delivered 123 clicks, six download clicks, and 72 website sessions. Exact-match [password manager] accounted for 110 of 123 clicks and 5 of 6 download clicks. The strongest headlines were “No Master Password” and “Unlock With Face ID.”
YouTube UGC YouTube Shorts generated 181 website sessions and five download clicks, making it the strongest tagged website traffic source this window, although it did not produce any Klaviyo-attributed installs. “Weird emails” led clicks (78). “Mom as IT” still led Click to Download (2 of 5), with “Airport password” also at 2. Performance was driven by the video concepts rather than the RSA copy.
