Direct App Store Acquisition

Goal: Drive app installs directly from the App Store.

We tested Google App Campaigns for Android, Google iOS App Store campaigns, iOS Privacy messaging, “Kill the Master Password” messaging, and Meta prospecting through OneLink.

Best performerDo not scale
Channel Google App Campaign (Android) generated 40 Klaviyo-attributed installs, followed by the Google iOS App Store campaign with 21. Together, they drove 61 of 81 installs. Meta OneLink and TikTok generated clicks but no Klaviyo-attributed installs. The iOS Privacy campaign produced 33 clicks but no installs.
Message On Android, “Check Your Passwords,” supported by the free-scan message, performed best. The “Introducing Privacy Mate” video generated 22 installs. On iOS, “You are the password—no master password to remember” was the strongest message. “You’re being tracked” did not convert. Meta Canva and UGC creatives generated clicks through OneLink but no installs. The “Jotting Passwords” video underperformed compared with “Introducing Privacy Mate.”

Cost efficiency: Android CPI was $3.32, while iOS CPI was $4.33. On Meta, “Your Face Is the Key” and the Privacy Mate UGC won on clicks, but those clicks did not translate into installs.

Retargeting

Goal: Re-engage App Store visitors, existing installers, ad clickers, and website visitors.

Retargeting spent $211 and generated no attributed registrations.

AudienceBest-performing channelBest messageWhat did not work

The alternative simple still spent $7 at a 0.15% CTR. “You Are the Password—Your Face Is the Key” spent $8.80 at a 0.27% CTR.
Existing app installers No channel generated additional installs or attributed registrations. TikTok drove the most engagement, with 448 clicks. If we continue TikTok, “Sync Your Devices” is the strongest click driver at a 3.4% CTR. “Airport Password” had the strongest six-second video-view rate at 10.8%. “Café” generated 155 clicks, but only 2.5% of viewers watched for six seconds. Overall, TikTok generated no attributed installs.
